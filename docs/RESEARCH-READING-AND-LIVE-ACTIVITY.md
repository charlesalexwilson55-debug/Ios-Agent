# Research reading and Live Activity status

The rendered reader now scans the complete readable body instead of the first
article or the first 20,000 characters. It retains article headers and captions,
and excludes scripts, forms, navigation and footer boilerplate. Login-only,
blocked, unloaded or image-only content is still not readable page text. This
does not bypass website access controls or implement infinite scrolling.

Research tries the rendered page even when a provider supplied extracted text.
If rendering fails, provider text remains available and the access limitation is
recorded. The reader checks the requested name before selecting a bounded model
excerpt, choosing the occurrence with the most supplied identity clues.

A first discovery can finish when a source matches the supplied name and all
context keywords, its attributed statements support the distinguishing clues,
and existing evidence contains one candidate group without conflicts. Soccer and
football, and NSW and New South Wales, are equivalent for page relevance. This
is a relevant source match, not a claim of certain personal identity. Selected
profiles still expand normally, and ambiguous or partial matches keep searching.

Chat has no navigation header. New conversation is in the plus menu. Hidden
reasoning and hidden activity labels remain hidden between research steps.

Settings > Appearance controls the Live Activity. Its compact presentation has a
silver capsule and a blue trailing dot. The expanded presentation and Lock Screen
show task status. iOS controls the Dynamic Island's system outline and placement;
the widget does not draw an unrestricted overlay around the hardware cutout.
Backgrounding pauses local generation and updates the status to paused. Live
Activities do not permit background Metal inference. Returning to an idle chat
or completing work dismisses the activity. No conversation or research subject
is included in the activity payload.
